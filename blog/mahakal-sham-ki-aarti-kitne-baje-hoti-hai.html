Mahakal Ki Sham Ki Aarti Kitne Baje Hoti Hai? Sandhya Aarti Time

AP Villas Journal · October 2026 · 8 min read

Mahakal ki sham ki aarti kitne baje hoti hai? From Tuesday, 27 October 2026, the Sandhya (evening) aarti at Mahakaleshwar Temple, Ujjain, is at 6:30 PM. Until 26 October it was at 7:00 PM. This winter timing runs until Phalgun Purnima, the day of Holi (Dainik Bhaskar, 24 October 2026).

If you are planning an evening darshan, that half hour matters. A family that leaves at the old time for a 7 PM aarti will now arrive after it has begun. Here is everything we know about the evening aarti, what sits around it in the day, and how we would plan the hours so that grandparents and children are comfortable.

Mahakal ki sham ki aarti: the short answer

The word "sham" means evening. At Mahakaleshwar the evening aarti is called the Sandhya aarti, the aarti of the twilight hour. Two Hindi news reports dated 24 October 2026 carry the winter timings: Dainik Bhaskar, 24 October 2026, which quotes the temple priest Mahesh Sharma, and News18 Hindi.

  • Sandhya aarti (evening): 6:30 PM from 27 October 2026 (earlier 7:00 PM)
  • Shayan aarti (night): 10:30 to 11:00 PM (unchanged)

The reports give a start time for the Sandhya aarti, not a window. We have not found an official figure for how long it lasts, so please do not plan to arrive on the dot. Reach the temple area well before 6:30 PM, particularly on Mondays, festival days and weekends.

The full day at a glance, so the evening makes sense

It helps to see where the Sandhya aarti sits in the day. These are the winter timings reported on 24 October 2026:

  • Bhasma aarti: 4:00 to 6:00 AM (unchanged)
  • Dadhyodak aarti: 7:30 AM (earlier 7:00 AM)
  • Bhog aarti: 10:30 AM (earlier 10:00 AM)
  • Sandhya aarti: 6:30 PM (earlier 7:00 PM)
  • Shayan aarti: 10:30 to 11:00 PM (unchanged)

Notice that the morning and midday aartis moved half an hour later, while the evening one moved half an hour earlier. People who remember the old routine often adjust the morning correctly and forget the evening. We cover the whole winter table in our post on the Mahakal aarti timings from 27 October 2026.

Why the evening aarti time changes

The temple priest quoted in the news reports explains that the daily routine changes by tradition twice a year: in Kartik for the winter season and again in Phalgun for summer (Dainik Bhaskar, 24 October 2026). So the timing follows the Hindu calendar and not a fixed English date. That is also why you will see two different evening times online, one for each season.

Why older pages say 7 PM

The earlier Sandhya aarti time was 7:00 PM. Plenty of travel sites, YouTube descriptions and booking pages were written under that schedule and never updated. If a page has no date on it, treat its timings as a rough guide only. Our own Mahakaleshwar darshan and aarti timings post explains how to read the different schedules, and the rule we follow there is the same here: the temple's own notice and recent local reporting come first.

What to expect after Holi

Once Phalgun Purnima passes, the summer routine begins and the evening aarti is likely to move again. We will not guess the new hour. When the date nears, check the official Shri Mahakaleshwar temple website or a news report from that week.

Sandhya aarti or Shayan aarti: which suits your family?

Many pilgrims ask which of the two evening aartis to choose. There is no wrong answer, but the two suit different families.

Sandhya aarti at 6:30 PM

This is the gentler choice. It finishes early enough for a warm dinner at the villa and a full night's rest. For elders, small children and anyone travelling in the cold, we usually suggest it. It also pairs well with a Bhog aarti in the morning if you would rather not wake before dawn.

Shayan aarti at 10:30 to 11 PM

The Shayan aarti closes the temple's day. It is a lovely, quiet time, but the cold night and the late hour are hard for some family members. If you are also hoping for the Bhasma aarti the next morning, remember that the shuttle drops guests at the temple gate by 3:30 AM for the 4 AM aarti, so a late night leaves little sleep. Our Bhasma aarti booking guide explains the permit and the steps.

How to plan your evening, hour by hour

Here is a simple plan we share with guests who want the Sandhya aarti. Adjust it to your family, and treat the temple's queue and entry rules as final.

  • Early afternoon: a proper rest at the villa. Winter days are short and temple floors are cold, so a nap helps the whole family.
  • About 4:30 PM: tea, warm layers, socks you can remove easily, and a quick look at the temple's current entry rules.
  • Around 5:00 PM: leave for the temple. Allow extra time for the walk from the gate and for security checks.
  • 6:30 PM: the Sandhya aarti begins. Stand quietly and let the elders take whatever rest the temple allows.
  • After the aarti: take your time, then return for a simple dinner. A kitchen at the villa means grandparents can have the soft, home-style food they prefer.

If a member of your family needs extra help moving about, read our guide to an Ujjain trip with elderly parents.

What to carry in winter

  • A shawl or jacket, since evenings turn cold quickly.
  • Socks that are easy to take off at the shoe stand.
  • Medicines and a bottle of water for elders.
  • Small change for prasad and flowers, and your phone for the shuttle or a ride.

Warm water for Baba Mahakal in winter

One more seasonal detail from the same reports: from Roop Chaudas, the day before Diwali, until Holi, Baba Mahakal is bathed in warm water because of the cold (Dainik Bhaskar, 24 October 2026). It is a small, tender detail that many devotees find moving, and it is part of the winter routine now running.

Frequently asked questions

Mahakal ki sham ki aarti kitne baje hoti hai?

From Tuesday, 27 October 2026, the Sandhya (evening) aarti at Mahakaleshwar is at 6:30 PM, according to news reports quoting the temple priest. Before that date it was at 7:00 PM. The winter schedule runs until Phalgun Purnima, the day of Holi. Please verify with the temple before you travel.

Is the Sandhya aarti the same as the Shayan aarti?

No. The Sandhya aarti is the early-evening aarti, now at 6:30 PM. The Shayan aarti is the last aarti of the day and is reported at 10:30 to 11:00 PM, unchanged this season. Many families attend one or the other, depending on how late the elders can stay up.

When will the evening aarti time change again?

News reports quote the temple priest saying the routine changes by tradition twice a year, in Kartik for winter and in Phalgun for summer. So expect the evening time to change again after Phalgun Purnima. Check the official temple site or a recent local report for the exact new time.

Why do some websites still say 7 PM for the evening aarti?

Until 26 October 2026 the Sandhya aarti was reported at 7:00 PM, and many guides and listing pages were written before the winter change. Where a page is undated, trust the temple notice or reports dated October 2026 over older blogs, including ours.

Can we reach the temple from our villa in time for the Sandhya aarti?

All five AP Villas are within 2 km of Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, about 6 minutes by car or 12 minutes on foot, and in-house guests get a complimentary Mahakal shuttle (up to 5 guests per trip). Still, leave early, since queues and entry rules are set by the temple.
