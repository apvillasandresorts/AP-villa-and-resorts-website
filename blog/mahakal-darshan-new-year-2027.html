Mahakal Darshan New Year 2027: Crowds, Booking and Planning

AP Villas Journal · November 2026 · 10 min read

For Mahakal darshan this New Year, expect the heaviest crowds between 25 December and 5 January, with 31 December and 1 January the peak. The temple has not yet announced its 2027 rules, so the plan below follows last year's pattern: online Bhasma Aarti booking was blocked from 25 to 31 December in 2025, and live viewing and moving darshan were arranged instead.

Every winter, families write to us asking whether they should bring parents and children to Ujjain for the first day of the year. Our honest answer is yes, with a plan and a flexible mind. We have hosted pilgrim families through several New Year weeks, and a calm visit is possible. For daily timings in winter, see our winter aarti timings guide.

What is announced for 2027, and what is not

As of 21 November 2026, we could not find any official announcement or dated news report on the Mahakaleshwar temple's New Year 2027 arrangements. So we are separating what is confirmed from what is last year's pattern.

Confirmed in 2026

  • Navbharat Live (15 April 2026) reports that the temple committee stopped offline Bhasma Aarti permission from 9 April and made online booking mandatory. The general advance window was cut from three months to one month.
  • Patrika (30 August 2025) reported that the temple allows 1,700 devotees a day to watch the 4 AM Bhasma Aarti, in the Nandi, Ganesh and Kartikeya mandapams, with seat numbers on virtual permits.

Not yet announced

  • The exact dates when Bhasma Aarti booking will be closed or limited.
  • Whether the paid quick darshan and the VIP protocol will run in the New Year week.
  • Gate, queue and parking changes, and the official crowd estimates.

We will say "last year" whenever a detail comes from 2024 or 2025. Please confirm everything on the official Shri Mahakaleshwar Temple website before you book travel.

Crowd dates: 25 December to 5 January

In recent years the temple has treated 25 December to 5 January as a special period. Here is what the reports show for earlier years.

What the numbers looked like

  • New Year 2025: the district administration reported about 10 lakh devotees in the city from 31 December to 1 January, according to ETV Bharat.
  • New Year 2026: Free Press Journal said around 2 to 2.5 lakh devotees were expected at the temple on 1 January, with 1,200 police personnel, 200 CCTV cameras and 5 drones deployed.
  • New Year's Eve 2024: Free Press Journal reported footfall about 25 per cent lower than the year before, which hotel owners linked to people going to the Prayagraj Kumbh and Ayodhya instead.

These figures were counted differently each year, by city or by temple, so please do not treat them as a forecast. They show that crowds swing a lot, and that 31 December and 1 January are the days to be careful about.

Our reading of the pattern

  • 25 to 30 December: busy, but usually more manageable on weekdays.
  • 31 December and 1 January: the peak. Allow much more time for every step.
  • 2 to 5 January: still elevated, easing as the holiday ends. This is our favourite window for families with elders.

Online booking and Bhasma Aarti changes

Bhasma Aarti is the 4 AM aarti where Baba Mahakal is anointed with sacred ash. We explain the booking steps in our Bhasma Aarti booking guide. Here is what changes in the New Year week.

Last year's pattern

  • In December 2025, Free Press Journal (18 December 2025) reported that online booking was blocked from 25 to 31 December because of heavy crowds and VIP movement.
  • Dainik Jagran MP-CG reported that the online booking stayed suspended until 5 January, with an offline system running until then. We could not open the full page, so please check the date with the temple.
  • In December 2024, Free Press Journal said online and offline registrations were suspended "as per the annual tradition", and live Bhasma Aarti viewing was arranged at Kartikeya Mandapam from 4:15 AM.
  • For New Year 2025, ETV Bharat reported a moving Bhasma Aarti that let devotees have darshan in about 40 to 45 minutes, with VIP passes limited.

What is different in 2027

Offline permission ended in April 2026, so the offline option used in 2025 may not exist this time. Navbharat Live also reports a ₹200 tatkal online quota that opens at 8 AM the day before darshan, and Sandhya and Shayan aarti bookings online at ₹250 per person. Whether these stay open in the New Year week is not yet announced.

The same report says the advance window is one month. If that continues, bookings for early January would open in early December, but please confirm on the temple site. Book only through the official temple site, and never pay unknown numbers found online.

If you cannot get a Bhasma Aarti seat

Do not let it spoil the trip. Regular darshan, the evening aarti and the open corridor are all meaningful. You can also watch the aarti live online. See our Bhasma Aarti experience post for what it feels like.

Best time to visit in the New Year week

If your dates are flexible, choose from these, based on last year's reports and our own hosting experience.

  • Best for elders and small children: 2 to 5 January, or a weekday before 30 December. Darshan in the early morning or late evening, with rest at your stay in between.
  • Best for energy and atmosphere: 31 December night and 1 January, if your family is comfortable with long queues and large crowds. Early mornings are cold, so carry warm clothes. We cover this in our Ujjain in winter guide.
  • If you want a quieter trip: consider arriving after 5 January.

Temple timings on special days are announced by the temple, often a few days ahead. Always check the day before.

How to plan your days

Two weeks before

  • Fix your stay. Rooms near the temple go first, and many families come for the same dates.
  • Check the temple's official site for the New Year notice and any changes to Bhasma Aarti.
  • Read our dress code guide, and keep phones and bags minimal, as the temple limits what can go in.

On arrival

  • Plan your arrival in daylight and rest. Our travel page covers trains, Indore airport and local transport.
  • Ask the caretaker to explain the darshan route and the day's gate arrangements.
  • Pick a meeting point and save one family number on every phone.

On darshan day

  • Leave early, dress warmly and eat something light. Carry water, and keep elders' medicines on hand.
  • Expect diversions and parking limits. Last year ETV Bharat reported four-wheelers parked on the Indore/Dewas, Barnagar/Nagda and Agar routes, so using a shuttle or walking is easier than driving.
  • Be patient and kind with staff and other devotees. The corridor and queue are a prayer too. See our Mahakal Lok visit guide for the walk.

Frequently asked questions

Will Bhasma Aarti online booking be closed around New Year 2027?

The temple has not announced 2027 rules yet. In 2025 online booking was blocked from 25 to 31 December, and in 2024 online and offline registrations were suspended around New Year. Expect something similar, but check the official temple website before you plan around a Bhasma Aarti seat.

Which days will be most crowded at Mahakaleshwar?

Last year's pattern points to 31 December and 1 January inside the 25 December to 5 January window. Free Press Journal expected 2 to 2.5 lakh devotees on 1 January 2026. These are not forecasts for 2027, so keep your dates flexible.

What is the best time to have darshan in the New Year week?

Weekdays between Christmas and 30 December, and the days after 2 January, are usually calmer than 31 December and 1 January. Early morning and the late evening are gentler for families. Temple timings change in this period, so confirm them with the official site.

Can I get a Bhasma Aarti seat if I arrive without a booking?

Since April 2026, Navbharat Live reports that offline permission has ended and online booking is mandatory, including a ₹200 tatkal quota opening at 8 AM the day before. In the New Year week seats are very hard to get, so treat live viewing as a good alternative.

How early should I book a stay for New Year in Ujjain?

As early as your dates are fixed. The 25 December to 5 January window is the busiest of the winter, and many families arrive together. Message us on WhatsApp to check availability. Our exact cancellation terms are confirmed on WhatsApp.
