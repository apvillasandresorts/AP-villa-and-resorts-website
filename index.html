The Destination

Ujjain — one of the seven Moksh Puris.

Home to the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, the only south-facing among the twelve Jyotirlingas. The Bhasma Aarti at 4 AM is unlike any spiritual experience in India.

Our villas place you at the doorstep of Ujjain's most sacred experiences — and within easy reach of its quieter ones too.

  • Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga — 2 km · 6 minutes drive
  • Mahakal Lok corridor — 2.2 km · open until 10 PM
  • Kal Bhairav Temple — 5 km · 12 minutes
  • Mangalnath Mandir — 6 km · sunset views
  • Ram Ghat (Shipra River) — 4 km · evening aarti
  • Bade Ganesh Ji Temple — 1.5 km · adjacent to Mahakal
  • Sandipani Ashram — 5 km · the gurukul of Lord Krishna
  • Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga — 78 km · day trip

Before you book

Questions families ask us most.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 10:30 AM and check-out is 9:30 AM, with one hour in between to clean the villa properly. Need a later check-out? Ask us, and we extend it whenever the villa is free.

Is it comfortable for elderly parents?

Yes. Our villas are wheelchair friendly with a lift, power backup, AC and geysers. Tick "travelling with elders" in the form and we will suggest the best villa.

Can we cook our own food?

Every stay has a kitchen or kitchenette with induction, microwave, mini fridge and utensils. We can also arrange a local cook for simple home-style thalis.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome. Please mention your pet when you enquire.

What is the cancellation policy?

For standard dates, free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Festival dates (Mahashivratri, Shravan Mondays, Kumbh/Simhastha) need full advance and are non-refundable. See booking policies.
