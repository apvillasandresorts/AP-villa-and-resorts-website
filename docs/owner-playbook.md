# Owner playbook — enquiries, bookings and reviews

Copy-paste templates for WhatsApp Business. Replace the [brackets]. Keep them as **Quick replies** in WhatsApp Business (Settings → Business tools → Quick replies) with the shortcut shown.

Every website enquiry now arrives with a line like `Ref: WEB-VILLAS-DIV-CARD` — keep it in your booking sheet's "Source" column (it tells you the page, villa and button).

## 1. Quote — shortcut `/quote`
> Namaste [name] 🙏 Thank you for choosing AP Villas & Resorts.
> **[Villa]** for **[check-in date] → [check-out date]** ([N] nights), [N] guests:
> • Total: **₹[amount]** ([taxes included / + GST])
> • Advance to confirm: ₹[amount], balance [at check-in / by date]
> • Check-in 10:30 AM · check-out 9:30 AM (later on request if the villa is free)
> • Includes: whole villa, kitchen, caretaker, complimentary Mahakal shuttle
> Cancellation: free up to 7 days before arrival on standard dates; festival dates are non-refundable. Full policies: https://www.apvillasandresorts.in/policies.html
> Shall I hold these dates for you?

## 2. Help me choose — shortcut `/choose`
> Happy to help! For [N] guests [with elders / children], I'd suggest **[Villa]** ([bedrooms] bedrooms, sleeps [N]) — [one reason]. You can see it here: https://www.apvillasandresorts.in/villas/[slug].html
> Would you like the price for your dates?

Villa page links: divinestay · ap-suite · om-shanti-stay · villa-aranya · rudrangan-suites (all `https://www.apvillasandresorts.in/villas/<slug>.html`). Groups: https://www.apvillasandresorts.in/groups.html

> Namaste! We're ready for you tomorrow 🙏 [Caretaker name] will meet you at [Villa] ([Maps link]). If you plan Bhasma Aarti, he will explain booking and the dress code tomorrow evening, and the shuttle leaves at 3:30 AM. Safe travels!

## 5. After check-out — review request — shortcut `/review`
> Thank you for staying with us, [name] 🙏 It was a blessing to host your family. If you have a minute, a short Google review helps other pilgrim families find us: [Google review link for this villa]
> Har Har Mahadev!

Get each villa's review link: Google Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** → copy link. Send only to guests who actually stayed; never offer anything in return for a review (Google policy).

## 6. Away message (WhatsApp Business → Away message)

## Booking sheet (Google Sheets, one row per enquiry)
| Date | Name | Phone | Ref (from message) | Villa | Check-in | Nights | Guests | Quoted ₹ | Status | Booked ₹ | Channel (direct/Booking.com/Airbnb/MMT) | Cancelled? | Notes |

Weekly: count enquiries, bookings and lost reasons. Close rate = bookings ÷ enquiries.

## Google Business Profile — one per villa you operate
1. https://business.google.com → **Add business** → name exactly as on the gate/signage, e.g. "Divinestay by AP Villas & Resorts".
2. Category: **Vacation home rental agency** or **Holiday apartment rental** (choose the one Google offers that fits; not "Hotel").
3. Address = the villa's real address; pin on the exact gate (fix Rudrangan Suites' pin first).
4. Phone +91 89990 61598 · Website: the villa's page (e.g. https://www.apvillasandresorts.in/villas/divinestay.html).
5. Add 15+ real photos (exterior, rooms, kitchen, bathroom), check-in/out times, amenities.
6. Reply to every review within 48 hours, good or bad, politely and specifically.
