# AP Villas brand brief (for blog writing)

## Who we are
AP Villas, Mahakal Collection: five owner-operated villas within 2 km of Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, Ujjain. Founded in 2023 by Apoorv in his hometown. We host. We do not aggregate.

## Voice
Taken from the four hand-written posts in `/blog`:

- **Warm and humble.** We write like a host who has welcomed many pilgrim families and wants this one to have a smooth, meaningful visit. "With warmth and humility, we share what three years of hosting pilgrim families has taught us."
- **Practical first, then reverent.** Give timings, steps and what to carry. Then leave room for the spiritual meaning. Do not preach.
- **Second person, family-focused.** "Your family", "your parents", "your children". Mention elderly members and young children often; they are a big part of our guests.
- **Quiet confidence.** "We have done this many times. We will help you do it well." No hype, no superlatives about ourselves.
- **First person plural** for the business ("we", "our caretakers"); a short sign-off from Apoorv is fine.
- **Indian English.** "Darshan", "aarti", "prasad", "dharamshala", "pandit ji", and lakh/crore where natural. Explain a term once for NRI and international readers.
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). One blockquote per post at most.

## Audiences
1. **Pilgrim families** (largest group): parents with children and grandparents, from across India and NRI families. Need: darshan logistics, Bhasma Aarti, comfort for elders, a kitchen, safety.
2. **Couples**: newly-weds on a first darshan, couples on a short spiritual break. Need: privacy, a calm base, a 2-day itinerary.
3. **Groups**: extended families, satsang groups, friends. Need: capacity, several villas close together, a tempo traveller, a cook.
4. **Simhastha 2028 planners**: families and groups booking early for the Ujjain Kumbh. Need: dates, crowd planning, why to book early, cancellation terms.

## Banned phrases (never use)
- "nestled"
- "hidden gem"
- "look no further"
- "in today's world" / "in today's fast-paced world"
- Also avoid: "unforgettable experience", "a must-visit", "embark on a journey", "boasts", "plethora", "delve", "rest assured", "world-class".

## Truthfulness
- Property claims come only from `content/business-facts.json`. If it says TODO, do not mention it.
- Temple timings, prices, dates, booking rules: cite a source and add "Please verify before you travel; timings change on festival days."
- Photos: real photos only. Topic photos (temples, festivals, ghats) come from Wikimedia Commons with a visible credit, or from the owner; villa photos show our villas. Alt text and captions must say what the photo really shows. Every post features **Rudrangan Suites** (with a real photo) in its "Where to stay" section; light, helpful marketing, never a hard sell.
